FGBG Privacy Policy

Version 2 — 9 November 2026

FGBG is provided by EUMENES.AI PTE. LTD., Singapore, UEN 202648582K. For privacy questions, access, correction or deletion requests, contact support@eumenes.ai. This notice covers the FGBG Android app, our service and fgbg.club.

The essentials

  • Your account stores your profile and meal data in our cloud. Diet preferences, allergies, targets, contact settings, food log, favourites, order history and learned preferences can sync between your devices. They do not stay only on your phone.
  • Shared shop knowledge helps everyone. Menus and ordering instructions learned or corrected through the app are stored centrally and made available to other users. Your personal diet profile is not a public shop record.
  • You choose your food and confirm your order. Shops and payment providers receive the information needed to fulfil your order. Scanning a QR or viewing a menu does not place an order.
  • Card numbers, security codes and shop passwords are not sent to FGBG servers. Where offered, saved credentials are protected on your device. Payment information goes to the shop or its payment provider when you use their checkout.
  • Optional voice, location, camera and Health Connect features have separate controls. You can use typed preferences and search instead. We do not sell personal information or use advertising identifiers for targeted advertising.

2. Voice and AI processing

If you use voice input, Android's speech recogniser converts speech to text. On-device recognition is used when available; a fallback recogniser may process audio through its provider. An offline preference does not guarantee on-device processing. You can type instead and can revoke microphone access in Android settings.

When you ask FGBG to interpret your typed or spoken preferences, the text is sent to our service and may be processed by our AI provider, Anthropic (Claude), to produce structured preferences. The resulting profile can be saved to your cloud account. Shop/menu information and relevant learning records may also be processed with AI. Do not include payment details, passwords or unrelated sensitive information in prompts or uploads.

AI providers and speech services may process information outside Singapore, including in the United States, under the terms that apply to the service used. We do not promise that all provider processing has zero retention. AI estimates can be incomplete or wrong. Confirm allergies, ingredients and cross-contact risks with staff; FGBG is not medical advice.

3. Shops, payment and other recipients

When you choose to order or create a shop account, your device sends the details needed for that action to the shop and its service providers, such as your selected items and the name, email or phone number you supply. Their own privacy and payment terms apply. Check the merchant and amount before confirming. Shop checkout may operate inside an app WebView.

Nearby place searches may send the search area to Private.coffee's Overpass service to retrieve OpenStreetMap data. The service receives normal connection information; its diagnostic logs may include a truncated IP address and request time and may be retained for up to 48 hours. See Private.coffee's privacy notice.

FGBG uses service providers for server hosting, website delivery, authentication, email delivery and AI processing. These providers receive information needed for their services. Google provides Google sign-in, Play distribution and Android services; map content is fetched from OpenFreeMap; website delivery is provided through GitHub Pages. Our server infrastructure uses Oracle Cloud. Information may be processed in countries other than where you live. Contact us for information about the safeguards and providers relevant to your request.

We may disclose limited information where required by law, to investigate abuse or protect rights, or in a business transfer subject to applicable protections. We do not sell personal information or provide it for cross-context advertising.

4. Health Connect and local security

If you enable Health Connect export and grant permission, FGBG writes meal nutrition entries from your food log to Health Connect. This integration requests permission to write nutrition data; it does not request permission to read your Health Connect records. The food log itself may already be stored in your FGBG account. You can revoke Health Connect access and manage exported records in Health Connect.

Where the card vault is offered and enabled, saved card data is encrypted on the phone using Android Keystore and protected by biometric or screen-lock authentication. The security code is not saved. Local shop credentials are separate from cloud profile data. Deleting FGBG data does not automatically delete accounts or transactions held by shops, payment providers or other independent services.

5. Retention and deletion

We retain account profile, food log, favourites and order history to provide your account until you remove them or request account deletion. Shared shop menus and ordering knowledge can remain after personal attribution is removed, because they describe the shop rather than your account.

Learning records and diagnostics are kept to operate and repair the service. The current retention process scrubs completed learning transcripts on its configured schedule (normally 30 days), removes direct account/device attribution from run reports on that schedule, and removes run reports after 180 days. Different records, including support, security, authentication and interaction records, can have different lifetimes. We retain information where necessary to meet legal obligations or investigate abuse; contact us for details or to request removal. We do not represent every technical record as immediately erased by uninstalling the app.

To request deletion, use Settings → Delete account in FGBG, or follow the account deletion page. Without the app, email support@eumenes.ai from your sign-in email with the subject FGBG delete my account. We may verify ownership before acting. Requests cover the account and associated personal data; we explain any information that must be retained and the reason. Deletion from live systems and expiry of restricted backup copies may occur on different schedules. Deleting the app alone does not delete your account.

6. Your choices and rights

You can change preferences, decline optional permissions, export account data and request access, correction or deletion. Withdrawing an optional permission prevents future use of that permission but does not itself erase data previously saved. Contact support@eumenes.ai for help, to withdraw consent or raise a privacy concern. We respond in accordance with applicable law. You may also contact your local data protection authority, including Singapore's Personal Data Protection Commission.

FGBG is intended for adults aged 18 and above. It is not designed for children. Contact us if you believe a child has supplied personal information.

7. Security and changes

FGBG release service connections use HTTPS. Access controls, signed shop recipes and on-device protections help secure information; no system guarantees perfect security. We update this notice when practices change and provide additional notice or request consent where required. The current version is published at https://fgbg.club/privacy/.
